Products / FluintyFleet

Fleet costs are no longer anonymous.

FluintyFleet collects orders from suppliers, including Inter Cars, and the manager assigns each cost to a vehicle in the app. You see what every vehicle costs as it happens, and alerts arrive before you overrun the budget.

How it works

From a supplier order to a cost on the vehicle.

  1. 01

    Order import

    Orders from suppliers, including Inter Cars, arrive on their own.

  2. 02

    The manager assigns

    Every cost gets a vehicle and a branch. 30 seconds in the app.

  3. 03

    Cost per vehicle

    You see what every vehicle and every branch costs, as it happens.

  4. 04

    Budget alerts

    Overruns show up before they happen, not a month later.

Why FluintyFleet

Costs fall when every purchase has an owner.

  • We block nothing

    Purchases go on as before. The only change is that every cost has an owner and shows against a vehicle.

  • Proven in the field

    The system grew out of a rollout at a courier subcontractor: monthly purchasing costs fell by 30%.

    Read the Art-Tim case study

  • Fleet in the same register

    Insurance and inspection deadlines are tracked in the same system, with reminders sent ahead of time.

Who it is for

Who it is for

  • Companies with fleets of several dozen vehicles or more
  • Courier and distribution firms, where costs scatter across branches
  • Fleet managers without a system of their own

How we start

First we show it, then we price it.

  1. 01 · Demo

    30 minutes on your data. You will see what the system does with your fleet costs.

  2. 02 · Pilot

    One team or one branch. It runs in production before you decide on the scale.

  3. 03 · Price
