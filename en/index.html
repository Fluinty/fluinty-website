How we work

Four principles we stick to.

  1. 01

    We leave alone what a person does better.

    Talking to the customer and judging the case stay with you. The automation takes what looks the same every time.

  2. 02

    We work with what already works for you.

    Your team keeps using email, files and Excel as it does today. If an off-the-shelf tool solves it for less, we will say so.

  3. 03

    If it does not pay off, we say so before the contract.

    Automation pays for itself where someone does the same thing dozens of times a month. We work it out with you, and we would rather lose the job than invoice for something that will not pay back.

  4. 04

    We start with one process.

    Only when the first one works and everyone is happy do we move on to the next.

FAQ

The questions we hear most often.

We answer the rest on the call, within 24 hours of your message.

How quickly can we start?
The audit takes two days. The pilot is running in production within 2–3 weeks. It takes three to seven weeks, depending on the scope.

Do we have to change our systems?
No. The agent plugs into what you already use: the inbox, the ERP, Excel, the CRM. If a system has no API, the automation clicks in it exactly what a person would click. We collect access and authorisations before the start, not along the way.

What if it does not work?
Every rollout comes with a 60-day results guarantee. If there is no effect, we fix it at no cost. After a failed attempt, the agent diagnoses the error and fixes itself, and a report goes to your channel.

Who owns the data and the systems?
You do. Ownership of the data and the architecture stays with the client, and we write that into the contract.

Where do our emails and invoices go?
The automations run on our own n8n server, not on someone else's no-code platform, and on request we set up an instance on your side. AI models only receive the fragment of an email or document that needs to be read, through the OpenAI and Google APIs, whose terms do not allow training models on your data. We get access through a technical account limited to the inbox and the system the process uses, and you can revoke it with one click. We sign a data processing agreement, and every read and every entry leaves a trace in the log.

What if the agent enters a wrong order?
The entry into the system is the last step. Before that, the agent checks that every field is there and refers anything uncertain to a human. Every entry has a log with a link to the source, so reversing it is a single operation. At Eticod since launch: zero incorrect ERP entries.

How much of our IT team's time will this take?
Usually one meeting and 1–2 hours: access to the inbox, a technical account in the system, sometimes a VPN. We do the rest. If the system has no API, the automation clicks in it what a person would click.

Tell us about one process.

Within 24 hours we come back with an automation proposal and the benefits worked out. The call is free and commits you to nothing.
