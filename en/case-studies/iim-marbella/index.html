IMPLEMENTATION 01 · AGENT FOR AGENTS

Every agent starts the day with a ready-made list.

The assistant ties together email, the CRM and the calendar. Overnight it reviews sales opportunities, wakes up contacts that went quiet months ago and prepares messages for approval. In the morning the salesperson gets a list: who to go back to, what to send and which meeting slots are free.

  • Email, CRM and calendar in one. The agent does not jump between tabs: everything is in one place.

  • Dormant contacts come back into play. Opportunities that went quiet for months get a second life.

  • A human approves, the agent sends. No message goes out without the salesperson's decision.

IMPLEMENTATION 02 · COLD EMAIL

A whole channel built from scratch.

We sell the client exactly what we use to sell ourselves. Domains, warmed-up mailboxes, prospect lists, personalised campaigns and reply handling, all on our side. The office gets conversations, not the legwork.

  1. 01

    Sending infrastructure

    Four domains and 60 mailboxes, warmed up so that emails land in the inbox, not in spam.

  2. 02

    Lists and personalisation

    Research on each recipient and messages written for that specific person, not a mass-mailing template.

  3. 03

    Campaigns and sequences

    Sending, follow-ups and variant tests, all run by us, with a report for the client.

  4. 04

    Replies go to the agents

    Interested prospects land in the CRM with context. The office talks to people instead of sending emails.

RESULTS

The channel is working, and so is the agent.
