The challenge

The knowledge was in people's heads, not in Google.

The advisers know the answers to the questions clients type into Google, but they had no time to write them down. The website drew 176 organic visits a month and was not gaining new keywords.

  • 176 visits a month. That is all the Google traffic the website was getting before the service started.

  • No time to write. Tax experts have clients, not an editorial calendar.

  • Topics went to waste. Client questions never turned into content that keeps working for years.

Process flow

The content loop: from topic calendar to rankings and back

A topic from the calendar goes through keyword research, a text written with an expert and publishing, and the Google rankings feed back into the calendar as lessons for the next topics.

The solution

A steady content cadence that works in the background.

  1. 01

    Topic calendar

    Topics come from the questions the firm's clients ask, not at random.

  2. 02

    Keyword research

    We check what people actually search for and what the text is up against.

  3. 03

    Texts written with experts

    AI writes the drafts; the advisers sign off on the substance.

  4. 04

    Publishing and linking

    The article goes live on the website, linked into the rest of the site.

  5. 05

    Rank tracking

    We see what made it into the top 3 and plan the next topics.
