IMPLEMENTATION 01 · AGENT FOR AGENTS

Every agent starts the day with a ready-made list.

The assistant ties together email, the CRM and the calendar. Overnight it reviews open opportunities, wakes up contacts that went quiet months ago and prepares messages for approval. In the morning the salesperson gets a list: who to go back to, what to send and which slots are free for meetings.

  • Email, CRM and calendar in one. No jumping between tabs: the agent sees everything in one place.

  • Dormant contacts come back into play. Opportunities that went quiet for months get a second life.

  • A human approves, the agent sends. No message goes out without the salesperson's decision.

IMPLEMENTATION 02 · COLD EMAIL

A whole channel built from scratch.

We sell the client exactly what we use to sell ourselves. Domains, warmed-up mailboxes, prospect lists, personalised campaigns and reply handling, all on our side. The office gets conversations, not the legwork.

  1. 01

    Sending infrastructure

    Four domains and 60 mailboxes, warmed up so that emails land in the inbox, not in spam.

  2. 02

    Lists and personalisation

    Prospect research and messages written for a specific recipient, not a generic mass mailing.

  3. 03

    Campaigns and sequences

    Sending, follow-ups and variant tests run by us, with a report for the client.

  4. 04

    Replies go to the agents

    Interested prospects land in the CRM with context. The office talks instead of sending.
