The challenge

The knowledge was in people's heads, not in Google.

The advisers know the answers to the questions clients type into Google, but had no time to write them down. The website drew 176 organic visits a month and was not gaining new keywords.

  • 176 visits a month. That is how much Google traffic the website brought in before we started.

  • No time to write. Tax experts have clients, not an editorial calendar.

  • Topics went to waste. Client questions never turned into content that keeps working for years.

Process flow

The content loop: from topic calendar to rankings and back

A topic from the calendar goes through keyword research, a text written with an expert and publication, and the Google rankings feed back into the calendar as input for the next topics.

The solution

A steady content rhythm that works in the background.

  1. 01

    Topic calendar

    Topics come from the questions the firm's clients ask, not from guesswork.

  2. 02

    Keyword research

    We check what people really search for and what the text is up against.

  3. 03

    Texts written with experts

    Drafts are written with AI, the advisers approve the substance.

  4. 04

    Publication and linking

    The article goes live on the website, linked into the rest of the site.

  5. 05

    Rank tracking

    We see what made it into the top 3 and plan the next topics.
