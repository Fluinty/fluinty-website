From a supplier order to a cost on the vehicle.

    Order import

    Orders from suppliers, including Inter Cars, arrive on their own.

    The manager assigns

    Every cost gets a vehicle and a branch. 30 seconds in the app.

  3. 03

Why FluintyFleet

Costs fall when every purchase has an owner.

  • We block nothing

    Purchases go on as before. The only change is that every cost has an owner and shows against a vehicle.

  • Proven in the field

    The system grew out of a rollout at a courier subcontractor: purchasing costs fell from 87 to 50k PLN a month.

    Read the Art-Tim case study

  • Fleet in the same register

    Insurance and inspection deadlines are tracked in the same system, with reminders sent ahead of time.
