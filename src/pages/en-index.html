Data and access

Your data stays with you.

Four rules that apply to every implementation. No exceptions for small processes.

Automations run on our own n8n server

Not on someone else's no-code platform. Logs, data and access are under our control, and on request we set up an instance on your side.

AI models only get what needs to be read

A fragment of an email or a document, with no history. We use the OpenAI and Google APIs, whose terms do not allow models to be trained on your data.

Access with the minimum scope

A technical account with permissions only for the inbox and the system that handles the process. When we part ways, you revoke it with one click.

A data processing agreement and a log of every operation

We sign a data processing agreement. Every read and every entry leaves a trace in the log, with a link to the source.

FAQ

The questions we hear most often.

The rest we answer on the call, within 24 hours of your message.

How quickly can we start?
The audit takes two days. The pilot is running in production within 2–3 weeks. A full rollout closes within 4–12 weeks of the decision.

Do we have to change our systems?
No. The agent plugs into what you already use: the inbox, the ERP, Excel, the CRM. If a system has no API, the automation clicks in it exactly what a person would click. We collect access and authorisations before the start, not along the way.

What if it does not work?
Every implementation comes with a results guarantee with a measurable condition, agreed before the start. After a failed attempt, the agent diagnoses the error and fixes itself, and a report goes to your channel. The condition is written into the contract. If it is not met, the final 30% instalment is not payable and we make the fixes at no extra charge.

Who owns the data and the systems?
You do. Ownership of the data and the architecture stays with the client, and we write that into the contract.

Where do our emails and invoices go?
To our n8n server, and to the AI models only the fragment needed for reading. Nothing is used to train models. Details in the Data and access section.

What if the agent enters a wrong order?
The entry into the system is the last step. Before that, the agent checks that every field is there and refers anything uncertain to a human. Every entry has a log with a link to the source, so reversing it is a single operation. At Eticod since launch: zero incorrect ERP entries.

How much of our IT team's time will this take?
Usually one meeting and 1–2 hours: access to the inbox, a technical account in the system, sometimes a VPN. We do the rest. If the system has no API, the automation clicks in it what a person would click.
